About Us

Expert installation, repair & maintenance

Temp Tech Heating & Cooling is based in North Ridgeville, Ohio. We install, repair and maintain heating and cooling systems for residential and commercial properties, and we are licensed, bonded & insured.

More about how we work
  • Trusted, skilled technicians for professional HVAC installations that ensure optimal performance.
  • Prompt, reliable repairs to restore your heating and cooling systems.
  • Comprehensive maintenance that keeps HVAC equipment running efficiently year-round.
  • Exceptional customer service and transparent pricing.
  • Peace of mind knowing your comfort is our top priority.

We take pride in being a trusted, reputable HVAC company that is licensed, bonded & insured, capable of any residential and commercial HVAC need.

Services

Four services, one point of contact

Expert heating and cooling, maintenance and installation throughout Northeast Ohio, for residential and commercial properties.

Air Conditioning Installs & Repairs

Precision cooling for your home or business, from system selection and installation to diagnosis and repair. Replacement of existing air conditioning and ductless mini splits are included.

Heating Installs & Repairs

Heating systems installed, repaired and replaced with care, from furnaces to boilers. Mini splits can also provide heating where they suit the space.

Indoor Air Quality

Better air begins with the equipment that conditions it. We install and service humidifiers and air purifiers.

Water Heating Solutions

Hot water systems installed, repaired and replaced, from standard water heaters to boilers. Both are handled as one service.
