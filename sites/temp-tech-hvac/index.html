About Us

Expert installation, repair & maintenance

Temp Tech Heating & Cooling is based in North Ridgeville, Ohio. We install, repair and maintain heating and cooling systems for homes and businesses across Northeast Ohio.

How we work

  1. Diagnose first. We find the cause before we recommend a repair or a replacement.
  2. Clear options. We explain your options and the pricing before work begins.
  3. Leave it tidy. Your space is left as clean as we found it.
More about our approach
  • Trusted, skilled technicians for professional HVAC installations that ensure optimal performance.

  • Comprehensive maintenance that keeps HVAC equipment running efficiently year-round.

  • Peace of mind knowing your comfort is our top priority.

Services

Heating, cooling, air and hot water

Expert heating and cooling, maintenance and installation throughout Northeast Ohio.

Air Conditioning Installs & Repairs

Precision cooling for your home or business, from system selection and installation to diagnosis and repair. Replacement of existing air conditioning and ductless mini splits are included.

Heating Installs & Repairs

Heating systems installed, repaired and replaced with care, from furnaces to boilers. Mini splits can also provide heating where they suit the space.

Indoor Air Quality

Better air begins with the equipment that conditions it. We install and service humidifiers and air purifiers.

Water Heating Solutions

Hot water systems installed, repaired and replaced, from standard water heaters to boilers. Both are handled as one service.

FAQ

Common questions

Straightforward answers, in brief.

How does requesting a visit work?

Choose a service and the day and time window that suit you, then leave your details. We call or text to confirm your time, and we will let you know if anything changes.

What if I have no heat or no cooling?

Call us directly rather than using the form. We work Monday to Friday, 7AM to 5PM, and we try to accommodate all emergency needs as soon as possible.

Call (216) 213-2622

Where do you work?

We are based in North Ridgeville and work throughout Northeast Ohio. Not sure we cover your address? Call and we will tell you.

Do you work on homes and businesses?

Both. We are capable of any residential and commercial HVAC need.

Do you offer estimates, and how is pricing handled?

Estimates are free on a complete system replacement. For all work, we explain your options and the pricing before work begins.

Are you insured?

Yes. We are fully insured, with $1.5 million in coverage.

Why does maintenance matter?

Regular maintenance helps heating and cooling equipment run efficiently year-round, and it is a good moment to spot small issues before they grow.
