Services

Two ways to light it up

Holiday lighting

Professional holiday light installation, so your property is ready for the season.

  • Installation. We design and hang your lights.
  • Takedown. We take them down when the season is over.
  • Storage. We store them until next time.

Permanent lighting

Permanent and semi-permanent track lighting that stays on your property, ready for any occasion.

  • Look good on holidays and every other night.
  • No yearly hang-and-takedown cycle.
