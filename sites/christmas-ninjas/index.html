What we do

Three ways we can help, for homes and for businesses.

Holiday light install, takedown and storage

We design the layout, hang the lights, take everything down after the holidays and store it for you. No ladders, no tangled cords, no attic boxes.

  • Rooflines, trees, shrubs, wreaths and garland
  • Installation before the season
  • Takedown after the season
  • Off-season storage

Permanent and semi-permanent lighting

Permanent LED track lighting is mounted along your roofline. Use it for the holidays, for events, or as everyday accent lighting.

  • Low-profile roofline track
  • Holiday, event and everyday looks
  • Installed once, no seasonal hanging

Why choose permanent lights

Commercial

Storefronts, offices, restaurants, apartment communities and HOAs. We work around your hours and your schedule.

  • Storefront and building displays
  • Seasonal install, takedown and storage
  • Permanent track lighting for brand and event looks

How it works

  1. Consult

    Tell us about your property and what you want. We confirm the details and give you a free quote.

  2. Design

    We plan the layout, colors and lighting for your roofline, trees and entryways.

  3. Install

    Our crew installs the lights on a date we agree with you, and tests everything before we leave.

  4. Maintain and remove

    If a section goes out during the season, call us. After the holidays we take it all down and store it, or leave permanent track in place.

Why semi-permanent lights

Hanging lights every year takes time, ladders and a weekend. Permanent LED track lighting is installed once and stays on your roofline.

  • No yearly install. The track stays up, so there is nothing to hang or take down each season.
  • More than Christmas. Use it for holidays, game days, parties and events, or as everyday accent lighting.
  • Clean look. Low-profile track follows your roofline without loose cords or clips.
  • Easy control. Features such as colors, scheduling and app control depend on the system we install. Ask us what is available. [CONFIRM PRODUCT FEATURES]
