# Images needed

Nothing here by default. No stock or fake imagery is used. Photos and logo must be the client's own files.
The page shows dashed "PHOTO PLACEHOLDER" boxes. To fill one, replace the `<div class="ph ...">` in `index.html` with an `<img>` (add real `alt`, `width`, `height`).

| File | Where | What to drop in |
|------|-------|-----------------|
| `logo.svg` or `logo.png` | Header (replaces the dashed circle; keep or drop the text wordmark) | Client logo |
| `hero.jpg` | Hero | Hero photo: a real photo of the crew, truck or a finished job |
| `service-1.jpg` | Services | Photo for service 1 |
| `service-2.jpg` | Services | Photo for service 2 |
| `service-3.jpg` | Services | Photo for service 3 |
| `og.jpg` (optional) | Social share preview, 1200x630 | Real photo; then add the og:image tag in `index.html` |

Tips: JPG/WebP, about 1600px wide max, under 300 KB each. Add `loading="lazy"` to images below the hero.
Delete the unused rows and fill in this table per client.
